What is Te·enah?

Te·enah is a symbol and a project built around gratitude, abundance, and remembrance. The mission is simple: help people share stories of gratitude — and turn those stories into a movement.

  • Meaning: Gratitude → Abundance
  • Theme: “By their fruits you will know them.”
