What is Te·enah?

Te·enah is built around a single purpose: a reminder that gratitude is a key that opens abundance. This page is intentionally simple—no donations, no story uploads—only the core meaning.

Prayer of Gratitude

EN

Thank You, Father, for life and for Your creation. Thank You for the family You have given me, for it is perfect, because You are perfect and Your creation is perfect. I love each and every member of my family, and I deeply thank You for their existence. I am immensely grateful that You care for us, grant us perfect health, protect us at all times, and provide us with everything we need and everything we desire. It is a privilege to dwell in this paradise You have created. I give thanks for all the people You have placed in my path, who have contributed to my learning and growth in this life. I also give thanks for the difficult trials I have faced and overcome through Your presence, which strengthened me to surpass them. For through these challenges overcome, I have found the truth. Thank You for Your goodness, Your generosity, Your mercy, and Your infinite love for me. Father, thank You for existing, for I live in You and You live in me. Infinite thanks.
